Equipment must be cleaned and the cleaning verified before it changes over from one product to another. The cleaning process is validated under protocol PROT-CLN-4.

The accepted residue limit after cleaning is 10 parts per million. Three consecutive successful cleaning runs are needed to validate a new cleaning process.

A result above the residue limit is a deviation and is recorded under [[sop-deviation-capa]]. Changes to the cleaning process follow [[sop-change-control]].
