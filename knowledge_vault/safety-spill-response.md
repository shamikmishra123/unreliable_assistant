Any spill of more than 100 millilitres must be reported to the shift supervisor within 15 minutes. The area is closed off and the spill is contained with absorbent material.

Cleaning up a spill needs full PPE as set out in [[safety-ppe-requirements]]. Spills of product are recorded as a deviation under [[sop-deviation-capa]].

The area reopens only after the supervisor signs the clean-up record.
