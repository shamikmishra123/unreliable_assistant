Gloves and safety goggles are required in the production area at all times. Gloves are nitrile examination gloves, SKU-017, and are changed every 2 hours or after any visible contamination.

Hearing protection is required in the packaging hall when the noise level is above 85 decibels. The PPE rules are part of protocol PROT-SAF-9.

Anyone handling a spill must also follow [[safety-spill-response]]. PPE use is part of the checks in [[safety-protocol-lot-verification]].
