Quarantine is used when a lot shows a possible deviation or a failed check. A quarantined lot cannot be shipped or released until a reviewer decides.

A quarantine request must include the lot ID and the reason. Quarantine must be applied within one hour of detection. Related deviations are recorded as described in [[sop-deviation-capa]].
