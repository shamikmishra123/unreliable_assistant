Every lot label must show the lot ID, the SKU, the expiry date and the storage range. Labels are checked by two people, and the second checker must be a different person from the first.

A label with a missing or unreadable field fails the check and the lot cannot move to release under [[sop-lot-release]]. Re-labelling is allowed only once per lot and is recorded with the names of both checkers.

Label checks are recorded against the lot ID. Terms such as QA and CAPA are defined in the [[glossary]].
