Audit records are kept for 7 years. Conversation turns are kept for 90 days and are then deleted. Training records are kept for 5 years, as set out in [[sop-training]].

Personal data is never written to the audit records, see [[policy-pii]]. Recall records follow the 7 year rule described in [[sop-recall]].
