A new supplier must pass a qualification audit under protocol PROT-SUP-3 before any of its materials are used. Qualified suppliers are re-audited every 24 months.

A supplier that fails a re-audit is moved to conditional status. Materials from a conditional supplier, such as the raw materials for [[catalog-sku-120]], need extra incoming inspection of 3 units per delivery.

Changing a qualified supplier is a change under [[sop-change-control]].
