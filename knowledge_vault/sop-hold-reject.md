A hold is placed when a lot needs more testing. The lot status becomes ON_HOLD until testing is complete.

Reject is final. The lot status becomes REJECTED and the lot is destroyed under the waste procedure. A reject request requires a reviewer decision, and a hold or reject request must name the lot ID.
