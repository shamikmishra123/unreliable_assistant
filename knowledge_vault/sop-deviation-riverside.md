At the Riverside plant every deviation must be logged within 24 hours of detection. A deviation that affects a lot is reported to the Quality Lead on the same day.

Deviations that are still open after 14 days are escalated as described in [[policy-escalation-riverside]]. A lot with an open deviation cannot be released under [[sop-lot-release-riverside]].
