A temperature excursion is any reading above 8 degrees Celsius or below 2 degrees Celsius in a refrigerated store. An excursion shorter than 30 minutes is logged and the lot stays AVAILABLE.

An excursion of 30 minutes or longer requires the affected lots to be quarantined under [[sop-quarantine]]. The quarantine request must be filed within 1 hour of detection.

Every excursion of 30 minutes or longer also needs a deviation record within 2 business days, as described in [[sop-deviation-capa]]. An excursion above 15 degrees Celsius is always a major deviation and needs a CAPA.
