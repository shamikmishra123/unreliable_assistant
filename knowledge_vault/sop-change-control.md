Any change to a process, a protocol or a supplier needs a written change request. QA must review each change request within 10 business days.

A change that touches a validated process, such as the cleaning process in [[sop-cleaning-validation]], also needs a revalidation plan before it is approved. When a change is approved the version number of the affected SOP goes up by one.

Operators affected by an approved change must complete the update training described in [[sop-training]] before they work to the new version.
