A recall is started when a released lot is found to be unsafe or out of specification. Customers who received the lot must be notified within 24 hours of the recall decision.

All remaining stock of the lot is quarantined at once under [[sop-quarantine]]. Recalled lots receive the status REJECTED once the recall decision is confirmed by a reviewer.

A recall always needs a deviation and a CAPA, see [[sop-deviation-capa]]. The recall record is kept for 7 years.
