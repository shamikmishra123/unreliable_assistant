Open deviations that reach 14 days are escalated to the Plant Manager. Deviations that reach 30 days are reported to the corporate quality board.

The Quality Lead named in [[sop-lot-release-riverside]] owns the escalation record. Handling of new deviations is described in [[sop-deviation-riverside]].
